# Demo deck outline

Rules: **7 slides at most** (title and intro count), English, a public link with no login. Going over makes the project ineligible for Global Judging. Slides must show the team name ("pironc"), member names and the challenge name. No NASA logo or mission insignia anywhere. Host the deck as a PDF in this repo.

Every number on a slide must be one the app shows for the demo field. Take screenshots from the running app rather than typing figures.

1. **Title.** Croppy. "Which rotation survives the next drought?" Team, members, challenge name ("Field Shift: Adapting Farms with NASA Data"), live link. Background: the NASA MODIS vegetation layer over the grain belt, July 2021 beside July 2024 (screenshot the app's map at both dates; credit NASA GIBS).
2. **Stakes.** Kazakhstan's wheat harvest swung from 11.8 Mt in 2021 to 18.6 Mt in 2024. Three northern regions hold 79% of the wheat area. About 1.6 Mha sits as bare fallow each year as drought insurance. The farmer's question: fallow, continuous wheat, or something new?
3. **What NASA saw on one field.** The 26-year MODIS strip with 2021 marked and the fallow year labelled "fallow, not stress"; the 2021 season curve against the field's own normal; the NASA POWER rain and heat figures with the diagnosis.
4. **The answer.** The three-strategy comparison: declared rotation, climate and water-resilient shift, soil-restoring shift, each with why, evidence, benefit, what satellites cannot see, and what needs local review. Include the trade-off number with its trial named.
5. **It responds to you.** Two screenshots: the priorities and "drier and hotter" sliders changing the ranking, plus the modelled-soil line.
6. **How it works and what it cannot know.** NASA MODIS, NASA POWER and NASA GIBS, with ESA Sentinel-2 for field detail, feed readable rules that produce the cards. Limits: climate is a 50 km cell, thresholds are indicative, the crop catalogue is unreviewed, no trend is claimed, satellites cannot see the crop sown or soil chemistry.
7. **Impact and next.** Who uses it; only the crop catalogue is regional, so other regions need a catalogue, not new code; next steps (agronomist review, Russian and Kazakh, Landsat history); data credits; the AI-use line.

Judging criteria, each scored 1 to 5: Impact, Creativity, Validity, Relevance, Presentation.
